Privacy Policy

KaysPay ("we", "us", "our") provides a mobile app for buying airtime, data, bills, exam pins, foreign phone numbers, eSIMs, verifying NIN/BVN details, and funding/withdrawing from an in-app wallet. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and how it's protected.

1. Information We Collect

To provide these services, we collect:

  • Account details: full name, email address, phone number.
  • A PIN you set for authorizing transactions — stored only as a secure hash, never in plain text; biometric unlock data never leaves your device.
  • Transaction records: what you bought, amounts, recipients' phone numbers, timestamps, and status.
  • NIN or BVN numbers, and the identity details returned by verifying them (name, date of birth, gender, phone number, and photo on file), only when you use the NIN/BVN Services or bank-transfer wallet funding features and only for that purpose.
  • Contacts on your device, only if you grant permission and only to let you pick recipients for bulk airtime/data sends — we do not upload your full address book anywhere.
  • A profile photo, if you choose to upload one.
  • Basic technical information needed to operate the app reliably (device type, app version, and error logs).

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process the purchases and transfers you request, and to credit/debit your wallet accordingly.
  • To verify your identity where required (e.g. bank-transfer wallet funding, NIN/BVN verification services), consistent with Nigerian KYC requirements.
  • To detect and prevent fraud, and to investigate disputed transactions.
  • To provide customer support when you contact us.
  • To show you your own transaction history and account details within the app.

3. Who We Share Information With

We never sell your personal information. We share only what's necessary, with the specific providers required to fulfil the service you requested:

  • Payment processors (Paystack, Flutterwave) — for wallet funding, withdrawals, and bank account verification.
  • Airtime/data/bills/exam-pin providers (VTU.ng, VTUAfrica) — to deliver the specific product you purchased to the recipient number you provide.
  • Identity verification providers (Prembly, CheckMyNINBVN) — only when you use NIN/BVN verification or update services, to check your NIN/BVN against official records.
  • Foreign number and eSIM providers (GrizzlySMS, Airalo) — to deliver those specific services.
  • Regulators or law enforcement, only where legally required to do so.

These providers only receive the minimum information needed to complete your specific request — for example, an airtime provider never sees your NIN, and a NIN verification provider never sees your transaction history.

4. Data Retention

We keep your account and transaction records for as long as your account is active, and afterward for as long as Nigerian financial recordkeeping and tax regulations require. NIN/BVN verification results are cached for 24 hours to avoid re-charging you for repeat checks, then only the historical transaction record remains.

5. Your Rights

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete your personal information, or ask questions about how it's used, by contacting us at support@kayspay.com.ng. Some information (such as completed transaction records) may need to be retained even after a deletion request, where required by Nigerian financial regulation.

6. Security

Passwords and PINs are never stored in plain text. Every transaction requires a fresh PIN or biometric confirmation. Wallet balances and provider credentials are handled entirely on our servers — the app itself never has direct access to move money without that step-up confirmation. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we apply industry-standard practices throughout.

7. Children's Privacy

KaysPay is a financial service intended for users who are at least 18 years old. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app's features change. Material changes will be reflected with a new "Last updated" date above, and significant changes will be highlighted in the app.

9. Contact Us

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to support@kayspay.com.ng.
