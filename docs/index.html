Security & compliance

Built for trust, from the ground up

Kay's Pay is engineered so that money and personal data are protected at every step, meeting the standards our users and partners expect.

PIN & biometric authorization

Every payment requires a fresh 4-digit PIN or fingerprint / Face unlock. A logged-in session alone can never move money.

Server-side money handling

Balances and provider credentials live on our servers, never in the app. Every debit is atomic, logged, and auto-refunded on failure.

Encrypted in transit

All traffic is protected with TLS. PINs and passwords are stored only as secure hashes, never in plain text.

Identity & KYC

Bank-grade identity verification (NIN / BVN) where required, consistent with Nigerian KYC and anti-money-laundering standards.

Data protection

We collect only what a service needs, share only with the provider fulfilling your request, and never sell personal data, in line with the NDPR.

Trusted providers

We integrate licensed, established partners for payments, telecoms and identity, so your transactions are handled by regulated infrastructure.
