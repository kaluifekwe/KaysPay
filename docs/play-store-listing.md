# Play Store listing copy

Paste into Play Console → Grow → Store presence → Main store listing.

Every claim below is checked against `service_controls` as of 2026-08-31.
**Before publishing any edit, re-check that table** — advertising a switched-off
service is how you earn one-star reviews from people who installed for exactly
that feature.

- Enabled: `vtu` (airtime, data, electricity, TV, exam PINs), `esim`,
  `foreign_number`, `identity` (NIN/BVN), `crypto`
- Disabled: `transfer`, `nin_modification` — **not mentioned anywhere below**

The previous copy promised "send money instantly to any Nigerian bank account".
Transfer has been off since 2026-08-29, so that line was actively recruiting
disappointed users. It is gone.

## App title (30 char limit)

```
KaysPay - Airtime, Data, Bills
```

29 characters. Unchanged — it already carries the three highest-volume terms,
and changing a title resets some ranking history.

---

## Short description (80 char limit)

```
Buy airtime and data with cashback, pay electricity, DStv and GOtv bills.
```

72 characters.

Why this over the current one:
- Drops "send money", which is switched off.
- Adds **cashback**, which is real (the data pricing engine credits it) and is a
  genuine differentiator — most competitors compete on price alone.
- Keeps airtime, data, electricity, DStv and GOtv, which are the terms people
  actually type.
- Deliberately avoids "cheap" and "cheapest". Highest-volume phrasing, but the
  pricing engine enforces margin floors, so it is a claim reviewers can argue
  with in public.

## What actually moves ranking, in order

1. **Reviews.** One rating, self-submitted, is effectively no signal at all —
   and it is the strongest factor Play uses. Getting to roughly 20 genuine
   reviews will do more than every word above combined. Ask after a successful
   transaction, when goodwill is highest.
2. **Install velocity and retention.** Play rewards apps people keep. The
   double-tap and refund fixes shipped 2026-08-31 help here directly.
3. **Screenshots.** They do not affect ranking, but they decide whether someone
   who found you installs. Screenshots with a short text overlay per benefit
   consistently beat raw captures.
4. **This copy.** Real, but the smallest of the four.
