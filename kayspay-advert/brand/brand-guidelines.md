# KaysPay Social Brand Guide

## Positioning

KaysPay is presented as a clear, approachable everyday-services platform for Nigerian users. Creative work should feel trustworthy, useful, modern, and action-oriented.

## Palette

| Role | Color |
|---|---|
| Primary | `#1A5C3A` |
| Deep background | `#0F3D27` |
| Light accent | `#D6F0E3` |
| Mid green | `#2E7D52` |
| Attention accent | `#F59E0B` |
| Text | `#FFFFFF` |

## Messaging

- Lead with one clear benefit.
- Use direct actions: open, choose, review, confirm, download, start.
- Mention availability, compatibility, eligibility, or terms when relevant.
- Avoid promises of guaranteed returns, instant success, universal coverage, or absolute security.

## Formats

- Instagram/Facebook square: 1080 × 1080
- Reels/TikTok/Stories: 1080 × 1920
- YouTube landscape: 1920 × 1080
- Feed landscape: 1200 × 628
